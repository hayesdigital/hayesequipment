Privacy Policy

Hayes Equipment, 1417 John B Hayes Rd, Ponca City, OK 74601, runs hayesequipment.com to support owners of the Hayes Products WSK-1 wireless remote start kit. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form, we collect what you enter: your name, email, phone number (if you give it), your generator model, the topic you pick, and your message. We use it only to answer your question or help with a parts request.

How form messages are delivered

Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service that delivers your message to our email. Their handling of data is covered by their own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run this website, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you've sent at any time. Just send us a message through the contact form or call (580) 767-9056.
